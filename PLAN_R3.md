# Round 3 plan: a whole game, not a test bench (D-105 to D-109)

Two builders work at the same time. Builder A owns the world. Builder B owns the screens.
The lead model makes the art (Blender, voxel style) and reviews.

## Shared rules

- Plain ES modules, no build step, no libraries. Keep files under about 300 lines; split when larger.
- Everything placeable or priced lives in `data/*.json`, not in code (modular, D-100).
- Talk between modules through the event bus (`on`/`emit`) and the shared state `S`. New state fields go in `makeState()` (`js/core/state.js`). Raise `saveVersion` in `data/config.json` once (to 3); builder A does it.
- Test with the no-cache server: `python 07_PROTOTYPE/tools/serve.py 8771`, open http://localhost:8771. Dev panel: backtick key. `window.__algo = {S, D, emit, on, skipIntro}`.
- Do not touch the other builder's files. Shared files: `index.html` (add only your own `<link>`), `js/main.js` (add only your own import and init line), `js/core/state.js` (add only your own fields). Put your CSS in your own file: `css/world.css` (A) or `css/ui2.css` (B). Do not edit `css/style.css` except to delete rules you replace.
- Game rules stay: TikTik and YouView only; no real brand names, logos, or OS logos; labels say what a thing does, never whether it is a good choice. Writing: short plain sentences, no em-dashes.
- When done, add a short section to `README.md` and a list of what you changed and what you could not finish to `PROGRESS.md`.

## Art contract (lead model delivers; code must work with and without it)

- `data/sprites.json`: `{ "px": 2, "sprites": { "<id>": { "w", "h", "ax", "ay", "fw", "fd" } } }`. Images are `img/v/<id>.png` at 2x game pixels. `(ax, ay)` is the image pixel of the footprint centre at floor level. Draw at `s = P(gx + fw/2, gy + fd/2, 0)`: `drawImage(img, s.x - ax/2, s.y - ay/2, w/2, h/2)`. Mirroring (flip) mirrors around `ax`.
- Projection: tile `tw 80, th 40`. One tile up is 98 px, so set `view.zUnit` to 98.
- If an id is missing from `sprites.json`, fall back to what is drawn now (old `img/st_*.png` or code prisms). Load `sprites.json` with a missing-file guard.
- Sprite ids (some may arrive later):
  - Stations: `st_desk`, `st_shelves`, `st_packing`, `st_filming`, `st_bed`, `st_algo`, `st_shelf_unit`, `st_studio_set`, `st_crates`
  - Decor furniture: `fn_plant`, `fn_sofa`, `fn_lamp`, `fn_rug` (rug may stay code-drawn)
  - Outside: `od_tree_round`, `od_tree_pine`, `od_bush`, `od_lamp_post`, `od_bench`, `od_mailbox`, `od_sale_sign`, `od_fence_x`, `od_fence_y`, `od_house_a`, `od_house_b`
  - Vehicles (drive along the y axis; `_p` moves toward +y, `_n` toward -y): `vh_scooter_p/_n` (courier with rider), `vh_van_p/_n`, `vh_car_red_p/_n`, `vh_car_green_p/_n`, `vh_car_yellow_p/_n`
  - Characters: `ch_player_front_0/1/2`, `ch_player_back_0/1/2` (0 = stand, 1 and 2 = walk). Front faces +x+y (toward the viewer); mirror for the other side
  - Items: `it_parcel`, `it_box`
  - HUD icons (`img/v/ic_*.png`, square 256 px, no anchor): `ic_cash`, `ic_trust`, `ic_followers`, `ic_energy`, `ic_clock`, `ic_day`, `ic_settings`, `ic_bell`, `ic_parcel`, `ic_phone`, `ic_bag`, `ic_pc`, `ic_rank`

## Builder A: the world (`js/ui/room.js`, `js/ui/outside.js`, `js/ui/layout.js`, new `js/ui/world*.js`, `js/sim/property.js`, `data/room.json`, new `data/world.json`, `css/world.css`)

1. **Camera follows the player (D-105).** The world is bigger than the 1920x1080 stage. A camera offset (smooth follow with a small dead zone, clamped to the world bounds) applies to the canvas and the SVG hit layer. Mouse wheel or pinch zooms 0.7 to 1.3. Floor clicks and `pointerTile` must stay correct under the camera. The camera eases to a station while its screen is open is not needed; keep it simple.
2. **Street block to explore (D-105).** `data/world.json` lists the ground (grass, sidewalk, road, crosswalk, lot areas) and props (trees, lamp posts, bench, mailbox, fences, neighbour houses across the road) by tile. The player walks the yard, both sidewalks, and the crosswalk; the road is blocked except at the crosswalk. Cars keep their lanes and stop at the crosswalk when the player is on it. Grass gets a light tuft texture drawn once to the offscreen shell. Keep the courier, sample rider, supplier van, doorstep boxes, and parcels from `outside.js`, using vehicle sprites when present.
3. **Property (D-106).** `data/world.json` `properties`: house extensions along +y (a studio room, then a stockroom), each with `price`, `rentAdd`, a floor rect, a doorway in the shared wall, and the stations it adds. Before purchase the area is a fenced lot with a for-sale sign; clicking the sign (walk there) opens a buy card (price, new rent, what the room does). Also sell them on a "Property" page reachable from the PC (builder B adds the site tab; A exposes `emit("property:buy", {id, ok})` and `S.property`). After purchase: walls and floor appear, rent rises (`js/sim/money.js` reads `rentAdd`), the room's stations appear. Studio room: a station `studio_set` that opens the same studio as the filming corner, with `effects.shootMult 0.75` while shooting there. Stockroom: two `shelf_unit` stations, each adding 2 shelves of 6 slots to `S.shelves` (keep `S.shelves` an array of shelves; each shelf station owns a range; the shelves screen shows the shelves of the unit clicked). Walls between rooms are drawn low enough that the player stays visible (or fade walls in front of the player).
4. **Furniture from the inventory (D-106).** `data/gear.json` gets a `furniture` list (plant, sofa, lamp, extra shelf unit) sold in the Tool Shop (builder B shows it; A owns the data and `emit("furniture:buy", {id, ok})`). Bought furniture waits in `S.furniture` (unplaced). Move furniture mode gets a tray listing unplaced pieces; pick one and place it like any station. A placed piece can be put back in the tray. Station-like pieces (shelf unit) work once placed.
5. **Art hookup.** Implement the art contract above for stations, furniture, props, vehicles, items, and the player (facing and a 3-frame walk cycle; stand frame when idle; carrying shows `it_box` above the head). Keep old fallbacks.
6. **Touch.** A virtual joystick bottom-left on touch devices (`matchMedia("(pointer: coarse)")`) that drives walking like WASD. Tapping the floor still walks.
7. Save version to 3. A new game places the player at the start; old saves reset cleanly.

## Builder B: the screens (`js/ui/phone.js` and new `js/ui/phone/*`, `js/ui/browser.js` and new `js/ui/pc/*`, `js/ui/hud.js`, new `js/ui/inventory.js`, `js/ui/sites/*`, the title screen in `js/main.js` `showTitle`, `css/ui2.css`)

1. **Phone (D-107).** A real phone: rounded body with a bezel and a small camera notch, a status bar (game clock, signal, battery), a home screen with app icons in a grid, and a home bar. Apps: Messages (the inbox), TikTik and YouView (each its own feed app in that site's accent colours), Orders, Bank (wallet and rent), Calendar (today's timed things: supplier arrival, courier pickups, rent day, known market events from `S.events` that already showed a signal), Playbook (flag). Tapping an app opens it full-screen inside the phone with a back arrow; the home bar returns home. Unread badges on app icons. It slides up from the bottom right, sized like a phone held in the hand (about 430 by 860 px at 1920). `phone:open {tab}` maps the old tab ids to apps.
2. **Desk PC (D-107, D-76).** A desktop computer screen inside a monitor frame: a wallpaper, desktop icons in a column (Browser, Seller Hub, Supplier, Tool Shop, Property, Stock Sheet), a taskbar with an own-logo start button (an "A" badge, no real OS logo), open-window buttons, and a clock. Windows have a title bar with minimise, maximise, and close, can be dragged inside the screen, and several can be open at once. The browser window has tabs and an address bar with made-up addresses (for example `tiktik.ph/studio`, `youview.ph`, `sellerhub.ph`, `supply.ph`, `toolshop.ph`, `homes.ph`). The existing site pages (`js/ui/sites/*`) render inside the browser unchanged where possible. Stock Sheet is a spreadsheet-looking window of stock: product, on shelves, in boxes, on the way, cost, price. A "Property" site lists the extensions from `D.world.properties` with Buy buttons that emit `property:buy` (builder A handles it; show a toast if `ok` stays false). The Tool Shop also lists `D.gear.furniture` with Buy buttons that emit `furniture:buy`. `open:browser {tab}` and the intro spotlight selectors (`js/ui/algo.js`, the supplier order button `.bview .btn.b-gold.sm`) must keep working; update the selector in `algo.js` if you change markup.
3. **Inventory (D-108, D-89).** Always-on bar bottom-centre: what the player holds (picked orders with their numbers, a carried box, parcels ready), small and readable. Bag panel on key I or a bag button: tabs Orders (status, number, product), Stock (per product: on shelves, in boxes on the floor, at the door, on the way with arrival time), Furniture (unplaced pieces, with "Place" that starts Move furniture with that piece: `emit("layout:place", {id})`), Gear (owned equipment).
4. **HUD restyle (D-109)** to the artist's mockup: chunky orange-gradient pills with a blue rim and a round icon badge overlapping the left end (cash, followers, trust, energy bar), a day pill and a clock pill top right, round settings and bell buttons (bell opens the phone on Messages; settings holds sound, full screen, new game), Parcel and Phone buttons on the right edge (Parcel opens the bag panel). Use `img/v/ic_*.png` when present, emoji otherwise. Keep the goal line. Keep the Seller Rank out for now (it is not built).
5. **Title screen** in the same style: big "ALGO SAYS" logo text (Lilita One, gold fill, navy stroke, slight 3D drop), a sky-blue background, the start cards.
